Inheritance and asset tracing

01Tracing assets and inheritance rights in a family estate

A widow contacted the firm after the death of her father-in-law, concerned that information about the division of the estate was being withheld by her brother-in-law. A comprehensive financial investigation revealed that her late husband's brother had been managing the grandfather's apartment and collecting rent on it for years — a property valued at over ILS 5 million.

Field
Financial investigations and asset tracing
Scope
Family / private

Background

After the death of her husband, and later of his father, the question of dividing the inheritance arose between the client — as the son's widow — and her husband's brother. A sense that details were being hidden and a lack of transparency on the brother-in-law's part led the client to contact the firm to find out the real situation regarding the estate's assets.

Objective

To check whether there were assets and income connected to the estate that had not been disclosed to the client, and to give her a clear financial picture for establishing her share and her rights.

What we did

We carried out a comprehensive financial investigation into the conduct and rights surrounding the estate's assets and the value of the relevant property.

Key findings

The investigation found that several years earlier, the late husband's brother had begun managing the apartment that belonged to the grandfather and had even been receiving rent payments for it. The calculation and valuation showed that the apartment is worth over ILS 5 million, and that half of it clearly belongs to the client.

Outcome

The client received a clear, well-founded picture of how the apartment was managed and of its value, allowing her to pursue her share of the inheritance with confidence.

"This apartment changes the whole picture — suddenly I have real strength and a real basis to give my children support and security for the future."

— The client, after receiving the findings
Inventory loss and risk

02Inventory loss investigation – uncovering a technical failure and clearing suspicion

A leading beverage company faced the consistent, large-scale disappearance of alcohol stock, with no witnesses and no clear leads. The firm was asked to carry out a comprehensive investigation to find the source of the failure, provide an accurate picture of the situation and restore professional calm to the organization.

Field
Risk management and complex investigations
Scope
National (production plant)

Background

A leading beverage company discovered that a significant quantity of alcohol products had disappeared from its production cycles with no clear explanation. Beyond the serious financial exposure, the uncertainty created a heavy burden and growing suspicion towards the staff and management, which threatened to badly damage the relationships and organizational culture built over many years.

Objective

To identify the source of the inventory loss conclusively and independently, to determine whether it was a human factor or a system failure, and to give management conclusive, well-founded evidence so it could make decisions with peace of mind and full certainty.

What we did

We carried out a systematic reconstruction of events and an end-to-end review of every stage of production. The work included analyzing operational data across multiple production cycles, together with meticulous physical and technological inspections of the work environment.

Key findings

The investigation entirely ruled out any human or criminal involvement by the employees. The technical inspection found two faulty filling heads on the bottling machine that caused a hidden, consistent leak during routine work breaks. Because of the structure of the production floor, the leaked liquid was absorbed and drained away directly without leaving visible traces, which added up to a significant numerical gap over months.

Outcome

Once the well-founded findings were presented, the source of the financial loss was exposed and dealt with immediately. The most significant result for the owners was lifting the heavy suspicion from the company's people and restoring full trust and professional calm, a testament to the firm's guiding principle: not every investigation ends in blame, and sometimes the greatest achievement is proving people's innocence beyond doubt.

"The relief was immediate. The investigation report not only stopped the financial bleeding, it above all lifted a heavy cloud of suspicion and restored my full trust in my staff."

— The client's conclusion after receiving the report
Internal theft and employee integrity

03Internal theft investigation – exposing systematic embezzlement in the logistics operation

A large company operating an e-commerce and consumer club discovered that a mobile device had disappeared from its central warehouse. What first looked like an isolated incident soon raised suspicion of a systematic, ongoing scheme. We were asked to carry out a comprehensive, focused investigation to identify those responsible and expose how it worked.

Field
Employee integrity and risk management
Scope
National (headquarters and logistics)

Background

The disappearance of a single mobile device from the central warehouse of an e-commerce company raised the suspicion that this was not a random incident but a systematic scheme that had been concealed for many months. The main challenge was the complete absence of official inventory records for the missing goods, which made it impossible to find the source of the failure by conventional means.

Objective

To narrow the suspicion down from a list of eight employees with access to the warehouse, to carry out a thorough in-depth inquiry, and to establish conclusive, unequivocal evidence of who was behind the missing stock and how they operated.

What we did

The firm's team carried out extensive intelligence and technological research (OSINT) on all the suspects, cross-referencing data and digital activity. These indicators narrowed the focus to just two key employees. All those involved then took part in strategic, structured interviews, supported by professional polygraph tests to corroborate the findings.

Key findings

The investigation revealed that the logistics team leader, who held full authority and a sensitive position of trust over goods coming in and out, had abused his role to systematically intercept new devices as soon as they arrived, before they were registered in the company's inventory systems. The devices were sold privately without leaving a paper trail, while the receiving documents were completely suppressed.

Outcome

Thanks to the combination of advanced digital intelligence and focused face-to-face interviews, the systematic embezzlement was fully exposed and stopped completely. The company's management received a conclusive findings report allowing it to take full legal and disciplinary action, together with recommendations to close the security gaps in the logistics operation.

"Learning that the person responsible for preventing loss was the one running the embezzlement was hard, but the precise report we received gave us conclusive evidence, peace of mind and the ability to take legal action immediately."

— The client's conclusion after receiving the report
Business disputes and competitive intelligence

04Business dispute investigation – exposing a conflict of interest and the steering of projects in the real estate market

A real estate development company kept running into obstacles and was unable to win and carry out projects in a particular town, because of opposition from an influential municipal figure. The firm was asked to carry out a comprehensive investigation to understand the motives behind these obstacles and to examine whether outside considerations or hidden interests lay behind the decisions.

Field
Competitive intelligence and OSINT investigations
Scope
National (real estate sector and local authorities)

Background

A leading real estate company found itself repeatedly shut out of business opportunities and projects in a certain town, because of what looked like consistent, deliberate opposition from a local elected official. The company needed to find the real reason for these obstacles, to understand whether it was facing fair competition or unlawful activity preventing it from operating in the area.

Objective

To map the full range of the subject's connections, affiliations and business activity, in order to identify hidden personal or commercial interests that could explain his opposition to the real estate company and the blocking of its projects in the area.

What we did

The firm's team carried out an in-depth open-source intelligence (OSINT) investigation and searched commercial and financial databases. The investigation included cross-referencing business registrations, analyzing digital connections, official committee minutes and media presence, together with a close examination of the bodies and companies operating and carrying out public projects in the same geographical area.

Key findings

The investigation revealed that the elected official was acting unlawfully and in a serious conflict of interest: alongside his public role, which influenced the approval of projects and infrastructure, he was in practice working as a developer for a company in direct competition with the real estate company that commissioned the work. It also emerged that his ties with the competing company were close and included direct involvement in projects in the area, while no public declaration of a conflict of interest had been made about this activity, as the law requires.

Outcome

The firm's report gave the real estate company the "smoking gun": conclusive, evidence-based proof that the professional obstacles it faced stemmed from an outside business interest and from the subject's unlawful activity on behalf of the competing company. This strategic information gave the company a decisive legal and business advantage in the dispute, and opened the way for it to act through the relevant administrative and legal channels to remove the obstacles and protect its rights.

"Thanks to the firm's report, we understood that we had not lost a fair competition, but had been blocked by someone acting in a serious conflict of interest on behalf of our competitors. The evidence we received completely changed the balance of power in the dispute."

— The client's conclusion after receiving the report

Accessibility level: AA (Israeli Standard IS 5568)

1. Accessibility policy

We believe in equal rights and in every person's right to receive information and services independently and equally. This website was designed and built with particular emphasis on accessibility for people with disabilities.

2. Accessibility features on the website

  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Screen reader support
  • Appropriate color contrast
  • Flexible, resizable font sizes
  • Detailed image descriptions (alt text)
  • Hierarchically structured headings
  • Descriptive, clear links
  • Zoom up to 200% without loss of content

WCAG 2.1 compliance

The website complies with level AA of the WCAG 2.1 standard, which includes:

  • Perceivable: information is presented in ways users can perceive
  • Operable: interface components can be operated
  • Understandable: information and the user interface are understandable
  • Robust: content can be interpreted by a wide range of technologies

3. Supported assistive technologies

  • NVDA (Windows)
  • JAWS (Windows)
  • VoiceOver (Mac/iOS)
  • TalkBack (Android)
  • Dragon NaturallySpeaking
  • Switch navigation

4. Accessibility testing

The website was tested using:

  • Automated accessibility testing tools
  • Manual testing with screen readers
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Color contrast checks
  • Testing with users with disabilities

5. Ongoing improvements

We continually work to improve the website's accessibility and update it in line with technological developments and user feedback.
